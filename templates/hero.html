How it works

Four quiet layers, one gentle signal

Each layer is cheap, local, and humble. Nothing surfaces until a real pattern forms across days — so you're never alarmed over a single hard night.

Layer 1
Keyword scan
A local word list catches obvious signals instantly. No AI, no network.
Layer 2
Semantic context
A model reads meaning, not just words — "what's even the point" lands like it should.
Layer 3
Behavioral rhythm
Tab-switching, typing pace, late nights — measured against your own baseline. Reads no text.
Layer 4
Trend gate
A single bad day means nothing. Only a sustained pattern can surface a moment.
The science

Your baseline is the truth

SilentHelp doesn't compare you to anyone else. It learns your normal — when you focus, how you type, when you wind down — and watches for drift away from it. Deviation from your own rhythm is the signal, not some universal threshold. The model only ever flags; plain, auditable code decides what you see, and a crisis phrase is escalated even if the model hesitates.

SilentHelp is not a medical product and does not diagnose any condition. It surfaces patterns and points you toward real human support.
