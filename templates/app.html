HOW YOUR PRIVACY WORKS
🔒
What SilentHelp looks at
Only text you actively write (chat, or the focused field / screen when the Mac agent is on) and rhythm signals — tab switches, typing corrections, late-night minutes. Never your files, photos, passwords, or browsing history.
💾
What gets stored
Only the signal — a category and a severity level with a timestamp. The sentences that triggered a moment are never saved. Your chat history stays in your own account file, readable by no one else.
☁️
What ever leaves the device
One thing, and only if Layer 2 is on: the text being checked is sent to the semantic model for meaning-reading, then discarded — it is not used for training and not stored by us. Turn Layer 2 off and nothing leaves at all.
✉️
Who ever gets told
No one — unless all four layers cross a sustained threshold, and even then a note is only drafted for your trusted contact. You read it, you edit it, you press send. Nothing is ever sent silently.
🗑️
Your right to disappear
Export everything as one file, or delete everything permanently, below — no copies, no "retention window", no questions.

How to install the Mac app
Two minutes, one time. The agent then watches quietly from your menu bar.
1
Click Download for Mac above — you'll get SilentHelpAgent.zip. Double-click it to unzip.
2
Drag SilentHelpAgent.app into your Applications folder.
3
Right-click the app → Open → Open (first launch only — this tells macOS you trust it).
4
Grant the two permissions it asks for in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security: Accessibility (reads the focused text field) and Screen Recording (lets it see Google Docs & browsers). Flip the toggle next to SilentHelpAgent in each.
5
Look for 🟢 SH in your menu bar — green means it's watching over you. From that menu you can send a test alert, or Ignore for 1–2 hours when you want quiet.
If the icon stays 🔴 red, re-open System Settings and check both permissions. Everything the agent reads stays on your Mac.
