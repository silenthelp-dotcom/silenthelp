The team

The people behind
the quiet layer.

Built by students, for students. Three high schoolers who think support should arrive before you have to ask for it. Est. 2026, built in the open, free for everyone.

Founder · CEO · Chief Product & UX Officer

Sree Saran Lakkaraju

Read bio

SilentHelp began with Sree's product vision: creating a privacy-first wellness system that can notice meaningful signals without turning support into surveillance. He leads the company's strategic direction, product architecture, UI/UX design, brand, and end-to-end user experience — from the first screen a student sees to the safeguards behind every interaction. Sree defines the core product decisions: what SilentHelp should detect, how it should respond, where it must remain silent, and how AI can improve context awareness without replacing human choice. He also directs product testing, feature prioritization, design iteration, and the long-term vision for SilentHelp as a trusted student-wellness platform.

Co-Founder · Lead AI/ML & Detection Systems Engineer

Abhinav Allani

Read bio

Abhinav leads SilentHelp's AI/ML and detection-systems engineering. He established the layered detection framework that combines deterministic signal matching, semantic context classification, behavioral indicators, and time-based trend analysis. His work focuses on making detection more accurate, context-aware, and resilient — helping the system distinguish casual language, humor, stress, burnout, and potentially urgent signals without relying on one-word triggers alone. Abhinav is responsible for improving model integration, classifier behavior, evaluation scenarios, routing logic, and the technical safeguards that ensure AI provides semantic insight while deterministic code retains control over every response.

Co-Founder · Detection Data & Intelligence Engineer

Nehanth Arvapalli

Read bio

Nehanth leads the structured detection-data foundation behind SilentHelp's recognition system. He develops and organizes the detection database, including phrase families, contextual patterns, severity groupings, signal categories, and evaluation examples that help the system recognize how people naturally express stress, isolation, burnout, and emotional distress.

Research Advisor

Naga Sai Dhiraj Kasam

Read bio

Naga Sai Dhiraj Kasam is an Engineering Physics student at the University of Illinois Chicago’s Honors College and a Research Advisor at SilentHelp. His interests span artificial intelligence, computational physics, quantum computing, and bioinformatics. He brings experience in interdisciplinary research, machine learning, and technical prototyping, alongside leadership in student organizations and community outreach. He is particularly interested in applying science and technology to practical challenges with meaningful social impact.
