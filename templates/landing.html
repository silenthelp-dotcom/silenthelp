How it works

It watches the pattern, not the person.

Most tools wait for you to open them and say something is wrong — which is exactly the moment that is hardest. SilentHelp works the other way around.

1

It learns your baseline

Typing rhythm, session length, late-night use, how often you switch away. A few ordinary days establish what normal looks like for you.

2

It notices the drift

Signals are read on your machine and judged in context — a joke about a hard exam is not the same as the sentence underneath it.

3

It waits for a trend

One rough night is a rough night. Nothing surfaces until a pattern holds — unless something urgent appears, which never waits.

4

It hands you to a human

When it matters, it drafts the message to a counselor or someone you trust. You read it. You edit it. You press send.

Privacy

The data never leaves.

A wellness tool that quietly ships your worst moments to a server has already failed. SilentHelp is built so that betrayal isn't technically possible.

  • ✓Detection runs on your device. Raw text is never stored or transmitted.
  • ✓Only a category and a level are recorded — never what you wrote.
  • ✓Nothing is sent to anyone automatically. Escalation is drafted; you send it.
  • ✓No selling data. No training public models on it. No ads, ever.
  • ✓Export or erase everything, any time, in one click.
