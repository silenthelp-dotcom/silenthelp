    By the numbers

    Who it's for

    Built for the Ones Who Go Quiet

    Real Moments. Real Signals. Support That Arrives Before the Ask.

    The student at 1 a.m.
    Studying, scrolling, not sleeping

    SilentHelp reads the rhythm — late nights, restless tab switching, typing pace — against your own baseline. Layer three reads no words at all. It just checks in.

    The one who jokes
    "this test is killing me 😂"

    Nothing happens. SilentHelp reads meaning, not keywords — so it never cries wolf, and students keep trusting it when it does speak up.

    The friend who got a text
    Help a Friend

    Paste what they sent. SilentHelp coaches you on what to say, what not to say, and when it's time to bring in a trusted adult.

    The parent who wonders
    Families

    Nothing is reported behind your teen's back. What you get is a teen with support that's actually theirs — which is the only kind they'll use.

    The school counselor
    Schools

    When a student chooses to reach out, the message arrives already drafted — with a category and a level, never a transcript.

    The one in crisis
    988 · Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

    When it's truly serious, a calm full-screen check-in surfaces real human help immediately. Crisis always bypasses the trend gate.
