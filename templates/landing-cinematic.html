Questions

Common questions

Is SilentHelp a therapist?

No. SilentHelp is a wellbeing companion. It does not diagnose conditions or provide treatment.

What does it store about what I write?

Detection history logs the category, level and time. The browser app and paired Macs use hosted processing. An unpaired Mac can use a local server.

What if I’m just joking?

The meaning check looks for context, including jokes. It can still misread a message.

Will my parents or school see my messages?

You can review and send a draft yourself. After three recorded crisis signals in 24 hours, SilentHelp can automatically email your saved contact with up to 140 characters from the latest message.

What happens when it’s serious?

When it's truly serious, a calm check-in surfaces real human help — call or text 988, or text HOME to 741741. Crisis always bypasses the trend gate, and explicit crisis phrases can never be suppressed by joke context.

What does it cost?

SilentHelp is free for students, families, and schools — download it or open it in your browser today.
