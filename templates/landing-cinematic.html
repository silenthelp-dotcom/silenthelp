Questions

Questions, answered quietly

Is SilentHelp a therapist?

No. It’s a warm companion that catches jokes vs. real distress — and never pretends to be a therapist. SilentHelp is a wellbeing companion, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or provide treatment.

What does it store about what I write?

0 bytes of raw text stored or transmitted. Only a category and a level are recorded — never what you wrote. Every message is checked locally, before anything leaves your Mac.

What if I’m just joking?

“this test is killing me 😂” — nothing happens. It reads meaning, not keywords — so it never cries wolf, and students keep trusting it.

Will my parents or school see my messages?

Nothing is reported behind your teen's back. When a student chooses to reach out, the message arrives already drafted — category and level, never a transcript. The message to a counselor is drafted — you decide if it goes.

What happens when it’s serious?

When it's truly serious, a calm check-in surfaces real human help — call or text 988, or text HOME to 741741. Crisis always bypasses the trend gate, and explicit crisis phrases can never be suppressed by joke context.

What does it cost?

SilentHelp is free for students, families, and schools — download it or open it in your browser today.
