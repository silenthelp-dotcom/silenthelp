01 / WHAT WE SHOULD BUILD

Useful possibilities.
Honest tradeoffs.

These are proposed capabilities. The prototypes below test the interaction and workflow; they do not establish clinical effectiveness or a live care partnership.

01 / VOICE AGENTVOICE GUIDE AVAILABLE BELOW

Support you can hear.

Start with a user-initiated guide. A conversational AI agent comes after speech and response evaluation.

PROS

  • Less typing for short reflections.
  • Can guide an agreed routine.
  • May make check-ins more approachable.

CONS

  • Speech errors can change meaning.
  • Audio may capture bystanders.
  • A human-like voice can encourage overreliance.
Try the voice guide ↗
02 / NEARBY THERAPISTSDIRECTORY HANDOFF

A practical next step.

Help users find real resources without pretending that distance alone establishes suitability.

PROS

  • Makes seeking support actionable.
  • Can surface local and remote options.
  • Prepares questions about cost and fit.

CONS

  • Listings and openings can be outdated.
  • Insurance and age eligibility vary.
  • Search providers receive location queries.
Build a directory search ↗
03 / WEEKLY REPORTSWORKING MANUAL TRACKER

Bring the week with you.

Turn an agreed care plan and user check-ins into a report for the next conversation.

PROS

  • Reduces reliance on memory at a visit.
  • Preserves the user’s explanation.
  • Shows completed, partial and unknown days.

CONS

  • Self-report is not independent proof.
  • Automatic sharing may feel coercive.
  • Reports need recipient-specific access.
Create a weekly report ↗
04 / WEARABLE CONNECTIONSLOCAL CSV PROTOTYPE

Context with a source.

Import selected observations, keep their origin visible, and treat missing data as unknown.

PROS

  • Can reduce manual activity entry.
  • Adds dated observations.
  • May reveal patterns worth discussing.

CONS

  • Non-wear and denied access cause gaps.
  • Platforms require separate integrations.
  • Sensor readings do not prove adherence.

02 / VOICE PROTOTYPE

Press play.

A working browser voice guide using fixed, nonclinical prompts. It does not listen, record, classify or generate AI advice.

Choose a short guide

FOR THE FULL VOICE AGENT
